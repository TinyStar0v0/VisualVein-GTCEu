## GregTech CEu Modern

VisualVein reads ore-vein data through the GTCEu API and loads GTCEu ore textures
from the installed GTCEu mod at runtime.

- GregTech CEu Modern: LGPL-3.0
- Project: https://github.com/GregTechCEu/GregTech-Modern
- GTCEu texture assets are **not bundled** in the VisualVein jar.

## Minecraft

Stone, deepslate, netherrack and end-stone textures are read from the player's
Minecraft client resources at runtime.

- Minecraft assets are copyright Mojang Studios / Microsoft.
- They are **not bundled** in the VisualVein jar.
